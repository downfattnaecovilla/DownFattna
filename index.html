Welcome to Tranquility

An Eco-Haven in the Soul of Deniyaya

Perched along Andaradeniya Road in Deniyaya, Down Fattna Eco Villa was envisioned as an authentic sanctuary where discerning travelers can unwind, recharge, and reconnect with nature.

Surrounded by rolling green tea plantations and the biological wonders of the Sinharaja rainforest ecosystem, our villa harmonizes rustic sustainable architecture with modern comfort. Enjoy peaceful mornings sipping freshly brewed Ceylon tea on your veranda, explore the natural waterslides of Patna Rock, and fall asleep under a blanket of unpolluted starry skies.

Frequently Asked Questions

From Colombo or Bandaranaike Airport, you can take the Southern Expressway to the Godagama / Akuressa exit, then proceed through Morawaka to Deniyaya. Alternatively, we can arrange reliable private taxi transfers or meet you in Deniyaya town center.

Meals are not provided at the villa. However, guests are welcome to use the fully equipped guest kitchen to prepare their own meals. Nearby local restaurants and eateries are also available for dining out.

Wi-Fi is not available at the villa. Down Fattna is a true eco retreat nestled in nature — we encourage guests to disconnect and fully enjoy the peaceful surroundings of Sinharaja.

Yes! Down Fattna Eco Villa features direct private access to our crystal-clear natural mountain river and natural swimming stream. The freshwater is pure and cool, flowing straight from the Sinharaja rainforest ecosystem with smooth river stones creating safe, soothing wading and bathing pools.

All our suites and cottages are intentionally designed as authentic naturally cooled, non-AC eco-retreats. Located in the elevated mountain terrain of Deniyaya, evenings and mornings are naturally cool and crisp.
